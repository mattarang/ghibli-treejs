# G

**Premise.** At 10:30 the sun stands up and to the left of noon; the words nearest it are heavy and wide, and each line further away is lighter and narrower, as a crown's far side goes into shade.

**Fonts and licence.** Monaspace Xenon VF (GitHub Next, US), wght 200-800, wdth 100-125, SIL OFL 1.1, via jsDelivr; fallback monospace.

**Colour.** Canvas #f2cf6e; primary #4a2f05 (8.2:1); secondary #7b500e (4.6:1, labels); accent #b5300b (4.1:1, the time and season, large only).

**Rules.** The time at 13vw weight 800 wdth 125; lines step down and right while weight falls 800 to 200, width 125 to 100 and size 4.6vw to 1.1vw.

**Signature relationship.** Weight and width as distance from the sun.

**Must stay if it continues.** A light source in the upper left; a diagonal weight falloff; the accent on time.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the demo's own UI words. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release). Ten-thirty is the working copy's time; the light source should follow the real hour.

**Into Round 2.** Carry the signature relationship into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
