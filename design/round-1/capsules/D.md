# D

**Premise.** Leaf fall: words leave the crown, swing side to side as they fall (the falling-leaf pendulum), and settle at the foot as litter that fades.

**Fonts and licence.** Fanwood Text (Barry Schwartz, The League of Moveable Type, US), roman and italic, SIL OFL 1.1, via jsDelivr; fallback serif.

**Colour.** Canvas #f0c9a0; primary rust #7d3315 (5.8:1); secondary ochre #7e4c14 (4.6:1, italic labels); tertiary litter #705137 (4.6:1).

**Rules.** Italic name 8.4vw; falling pairs at 2vw zig-zag between 18% and 58%; the litter is a small justified pile at the bottom right.

**Signature relationship.** A zig-zag descent ending in a justified pile.

**Must stay if it continues.** Swinging descent; settled litter; italic for what is still moving.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the demo's own UI words. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release). Contrast fixes moved the ochre and litter closer to the rust: the fade reads less.

**Into Round 2.** Carry the signature relationship into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
