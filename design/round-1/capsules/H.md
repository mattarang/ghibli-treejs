# H

**Premise.** Snow settles on the tops of boughs: each name of a thing is a heavy grey bough, and its value lies right on top of it, small and white, touching it; tiers step out to either side.

**Fonts and licence.** Mazius Display (Collletttivo, Italy), Regular/Bold, SIL OFL 1.1; iA Writer Quattro (iA, Switzerland/Japan), Regular/Bold, SIL OFL 1.1; both via jsDelivr.

**Colour.** Canvas #2e3b45; primary snow #f4f7fb (10.7:1, values, name, actions); secondary bough #93a7b6 (4.6:1, labels).

**Rules.** Bough labels 4.6vw Mazius; snow values 1.35vw Quattro bold with negative bottom margin so they sit on the bough; tiers alternate sides, inset 12-22%.

**Signature relationship.** Small white values resting on large grey labels.

**Must stay if it continues.** The snow-on-bough pairing; alternating tiers; two inks.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the demo's own UI words. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release). Mazius glyph coverage beyond basic Latin unverified.

**Into Round 2.** Carry the signature relationship into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
