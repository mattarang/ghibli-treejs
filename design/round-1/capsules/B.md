# B

**Premise.** Space-colonisation growth: a narrow trunk at the foot, limbs that step further out and grow wider as they reach open space, leaf values at the tips, roots below.

**Fonts and licence.** Junicode 2 VF (Peter S. Baker, US), roman and italic, wght 300-700, wdth 75-125, SIL OFL 1.1, via jsDelivr; fallback serif.

**Colour.** Canvas #dde6d3; primary bark #3b2a1e (10.6:1, names and trunk); secondary leaf #476e34 (4.6:1, values, crown and app name).

**Rules.** Labels at width 75 / weight 600; values at width 125 / weight 300; indent grows 7% to 39% up the page; trunk 9.5vw width 75 bold; old-style figures in text, lining in values.

**Signature relationship.** Width as growth: words widen as they climb toward the light.

**Must stay if it continues.** Bottom-anchored trunk; rising indents; narrow-to-wide width axis; two inks.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the demo's own UI words. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release). Indents compressed on phones.

**Into Round 2.** Carry the signature relationship into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
