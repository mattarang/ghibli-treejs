# J

**Premise.** The meadow is grown nearest first and thins with distance: the nearest thing is the largest and runs past the foot; each step away is smaller, lighter and fainter toward the far edge.

**Fonts and licence.** Ribes Black (Collletttivo, Italy), SIL OFL 1.1; Commit Mono VF (Eigil Nikolajsen, Denmark), wght 200-700, MIT licence; both via jsDelivr.

**Colour.** Canvas #23401c; primary #e2efb0 (9.4:1, name and values); secondary #a9c46a (5.9:1, near labels); tertiary #94ac6f (4.6:1, far text).

**Rules.** Centred; sizes 13vw (cropped at the foot) to 3.6vw, 2.3vw, 1.5vw and .95vw going up the page; the page clips at the bottom edge.

**Signature relationship.** Perspective by size: the name cropped at the near edge.

**Must stay if it continues.** Size falling with distance; the cropped foreground; three inks.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the demo's own UI words. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release). Cropping depends on viewport height.

**Into Round 2.** Carry the signature relationship into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
