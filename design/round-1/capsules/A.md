# A

**Premise.** The controls live at the edges and never cover the tree: the centre of the page is kept empty as the place the tree stands; everything is pinned to the four corners and the foot.

**Fonts and licence.** Junction (The League of Moveable Type, Caroline Hadilaksono, US), Light/Regular/Bold, SIL OFL 1.1, served from GitHub via jsDelivr; fallback sans-serif.

**Colour.** Canvas #0f1a14; one text colour #cfe3c2 (13.1:1).

**Rules.** Corners carry the weight (name and season at 5.6vw bold, time at 11vw light); measurements and actions small (1-1.15vw). Margins 6vw/6vh; the centre stays empty at every size.

**Signature relationship.** An empty centre framed by four corners of type.

**Must stay if it continues.** Empty centre; corner anchoring; one ink.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the demo's own UI words. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release). On phones the measurement corner drops to 22vh to avoid the name.

**Into Round 2.** Carry the signature relationship into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
