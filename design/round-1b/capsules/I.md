# I (second set)

**Premise.** A crown's outline is lumpy: the centred block of words rises and falls in line length so its edge bulges like a crown; the name, narrow, stands beneath as the trunk.

**Fonts and licence.** Mattone (Collletttivo, Italy), Regular/Black, SIL OFL 1.1, via jsDelivr.

**Colour.** Canvas #3d6b35; one ink #f2f6dc (5.7:1).

**Rules.** Centred; lines of one, two and three pairs alternate; section heads Black 3.4vw; the name 4.2vw wrapped to two lines.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the Shape panel's real labels and default values for the camphor tree, plus the season, time, actions and loading step. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release).

**Into Round 2.** Carry the premise into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
