# G (second set)

**Premise.** Leaves sway in the wind: every pair sits a little off the line, up or down by a leaf's flutter. Names are the moving leaves (cursive italic), values the fixed twigs (upright bold).

**Fonts and licence.** Victor Mono (Rubjo, OFL 1.1), Regular/Italic/Bold, via jsDelivr.

**Colour.** Canvas #cfe0c3; primary #24361f (9.3:1); secondary #486331 (4.9:1, labels and section names); accent #934527 (4.8:1, season, time and actions).

**Rules.** Baseline offsets -0.12, +0.10, -0.05, +0.14 em in turn; pairs inline with 1.8em gaps; line-height 2.1 for the flutter; name 6vw italic.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the Shape panel's real labels and default values for the camphor tree, plus the season, time, actions and loading step. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release).

**Into Round 2.** Carry the premise into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
