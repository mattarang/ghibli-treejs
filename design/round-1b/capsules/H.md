# H (second set)

**Premise.** A crown is thousands of marks of one brush at slightly different sizes: every value is a dab, side by side, each a little larger or smaller than its neighbour, with its name small beneath.

**Fonts and licence.** Cooper* (Indestructible Type, US; a revival of Oswald Cooper's design), variable weight 300-900, SIL OFL 1.1, via jsDelivr.

**Colour.** Canvas #b84227 (deep coral); primary #1e0d05 (3.5:1, large text only: name, values, actions); secondary #fff3e0 (5.0:1, labels and small text).

**Rules.** Values in three sizes in turn (2.8 / 3.6 / 4.4vw) at weight 850; flowing wrap; labels 15px beneath.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the Shape panel's real labels and default values for the camphor tree, plus the season, time, actions and loading step. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release).

**Into Round 2.** Carry the premise into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
