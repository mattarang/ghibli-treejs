# E (second set)

**Premise.** Painted against measured: the names (painted, a soft serif) end on a centre axis from the left; the measured numbers (a strict mono) start from it on the right. The axis is the trunk.

**Fonts and licence.** Coconat (Collletttivo, Italy) Regular/Bold and Necto Mono (Collletttivo) Regular, SIL OFL 1.1, via jsDelivr.

**Colour.** Canvas #3b2f2a (bark); labels #d9c3a5 (7.6:1); numbers #f4ead8 (10.8:1).

**Rules.** Two equal columns meeting at the centre; labels right-aligned, values left-aligned; section heads bold; name 5.2vw.

**Assumptions and risks.** Working copy is the Shape panel's real labels and default values for the camphor tree, plus the season, time, actions and loading step. Fonts load from GitHub branches through jsDelivr (not pinned to a release).

**Into Round 2.** Carry the premise into the demo's real controls (tree chip, Shape panel, sun and season dials, boot screen) without normalising it into a standard panel.
